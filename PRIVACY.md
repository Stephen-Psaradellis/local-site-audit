# Privacy policy - Local Site Audit + Pitch

Last updated: 2026-10-14

Local Site Audit + Pitch does not collect, transmit or sell any data.

- The checks run inside your browser, on the page you choose, only when you click the
  extension's icon.
- Your name, contact details, license key, daily audit count and last audit are stored
  only in your browser's extension storage. They never leave your device.
- Same-site link checks request pages of the site you are auditing, from your browser,
  exactly as clicking those links would. No request goes to any server of ours; there
  is none.
- No analytics, no tracking, no remote code, no third parties.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/Stephen-Psaradellis/local-site-audit/issues
